\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:summary}

This work has produced the following.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data.} A thesis set of 11,256 \co{} solubility points for 113 ILs, and a referenced dataset of 12,297 points for 109 ILs from 171 publications, each with a verified DOI and the measurement method.
    \item \textbf{Calculations.} r2SCAN-3c optimised structures and frequencies for the ions of both training sets, all verified as minima, with stored outputs. Six ions built from wrong SMILES and three with failed optimisations were recalculated.
    \item \textbf{Descriptors.} A corrected descriptor extraction whose orbital energies follow the published ordering of ion ionisation energies (rank correlation 0.82 for anions), where the thesis values did not ($-0.25$).
    \item \textbf{Validation.} Grouped cross-validation shows that the random-split accuracy of the thesis ($R^2 = 0.985$) mostly measures interpolation along known isotherms. For ILs whose anion is absent from training, the best models reach $R^2 = 0.89$--0.91 with the corrected descriptors, against 0.81--0.86 with the thesis descriptors and 0.78--0.80 from temperature and pressure alone.
\end{itemize}
The corrected isolated-ion descriptors are as informative as elemental atom counts, but not more. Improving on this requires descriptors of how the ions behave in the liquid and towards \co{}. Future work will therefore (i) benchmark the electronic-structure methods against coupled-cluster interaction energies of ion pairs, (ii) compute conformer-searched ions, ion pairs and anion--\co{} complexes and COSMO-RS $\sigma$-profiles and Henry constants, (iii) simulate the bulk liquids with molecular dynamics, and (iv) train models with uncertainty estimates, validated by holding out whole anions, before the screening is repeated.
