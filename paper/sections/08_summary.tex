\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:summary}

This work has produced the following.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data.} A training set of 11,256 \co{} solubility points for 113 ILs, and a rebuilt dataset of 12,297 points from 160 publications, each with its DOI and measurement method.
    \item \textbf{Calculations.} A pipeline from ion names to r2SCAN-3c optimised structures in ORCA 6.0 for 78 ions, with stored outputs.
    \item \textbf{Models.} Four regressors, of which Random Forest performs best on a random split ($R^2 = 0.985$, MAE $= 0.017$). Pressure, temperature and the anion total energy are the most important features.
    \item \textbf{Applications.} A screening tool that reproduces known trends for imidazolium ILs, and provisional predictions for two bio-based ILs relevant to Indonesian feedstocks.
\end{itemize}
The checks in Section~\ref{sec:issues} show that the descriptors must be re-extracted and the models revalidated with grouped splits before the results can be published. The corrected parser and a grouped-validation script are available. Future work will retrain the models on the corrected descriptors and the referenced dataset, and extend the descriptor set beyond isolated-ion properties.
