APPROACH

From ion structures to a solubility prediction in five steps

1 · DATA

Experimental solubility

11,256 literature points for 113 ionic liquids

2 · STRUCTURES

3D ion geometries

SMILES → RDKit embedding → UFF clean-up

3 · DFT

ORCA 6.0

r²SCAN-3c optimisation + frequencies for 78 ions

4 · FEATURES

248 inputs

Orbital energies, dipole, total energy, atom counts, T and P

5 · ML

Prediction models

Ridge, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Neural Network

Implemented as 20 Jupyter notebooks plus a small Flask demo app; data, ORCA inputs/outputs and the trained model are versioned on GitHub.

IL–CO₂ solubility · what I built

The whole pipeline in one picture. Literature data gives the target. Each ion is built from its SMILES, pre-optimised with a force field and optimised with r2SCAN-3c in ORCA. Those quantum numbers, plus atom counts and the temperature and pressure of each measurement, become the model inputs. Four model types were compared.
