IL–CO₂ solubility · what I built

Shuffling pressure destroys the model, which is expected because solubility is mostly an isotherm in pressure. Among the quantum descriptors the anion matters most, consistent with the literature that the anion controls CO₂ solubility. But be careful: total electronic energy mostly tracks how many electrons the ion has, so the model may be using it to recognise which anion it is. That motivates better descriptors.
