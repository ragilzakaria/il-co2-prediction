THE BASICS

Four ideas you need for this talk

IONIC LIQUID

A salt that is liquid at room temperature

Made of a large positive ion (cation) and a negative ion (anion). We can mix and match the two, and the liquid barely evaporates.

CO₂ SOLUBILITY

How much CO₂ the liquid can hold

Given as a mole fraction: 0.3 means 3 of every 10 molecules in the mixture are CO₂. It rises with pressure and falls with temperature.

DFT (QUANTUM CHEMISTRY)

Computing a molecule from physics

Density functional theory calculates an ion’s energy, electron orbitals and charge distribution on a computer, with no experiment needed.

MACHINE LEARNING

Learning a pattern from examples

Show a model thousands of measured examples and it learns to predict new ones. We score it with R²: 1 is perfect, 0 is no better than guessing the average.

IL–CO₂ solubility · sharing session

Four ideas so everyone can follow. An ionic liquid is simply a salt that stays liquid, built from a positive and a negative ion that we can choose. Solubility here means how much CO₂ dissolves, given as a mole fraction. DFT lets us compute properties of each ion from quantum physics on a computer. And machine learning learns the link between those properties and the measured solubility. R squared is the score I will use: 1 means perfect predictions.
